The Ritual Guide

Optimal scent throw and clean burn instructions

01
The Memory Burn:

On your first lighting, allow the melt pool to reach all edges of the vessel (approx. 2 to 3 hours). This prevents tunneling for the lifetime of the candle.

02
Trim to 1/4 Inch:

Always trim the organic cotton or pearwood wick before reigniting. A clean trimmed wick eliminates carbon buildup, flickering, and soot.

03
Repurposing the Vessel:

When 1/2 inch of wax remains, gently soften with warm water and wipe clean. Your vessel is food-safe and intended for espresso cups, vanity brushes, or planters.
